This release adds the **server version**. After you sign in, EM3 asks which version you want, and you can change it later in Settings → Version:

- **Server version (recommended)**: adds deadline reminders, sync across devices, automatic NYCU timetables and TA collaboration. Your Moodle sign-in token is stored on the EM3 server, encrypted with AES-256-GCM, so it can check deadlines while EM3 is closed. Your password never reaches the server.
- **Local version**: works as before. Everything stays in this browser, and EM3 never contacts its server.

Switching back to the local version deletes everything the server holds for your account.

## New

### Everyone

- **Take quizzes in EM3** (both versions): no more jumping to E3. Before you start, EM3 shows the time limit and attempts left. While you answer, it autosaves, shows a question navigator and a countdown, warns you at 5 minutes and 1 minute left, and submits when time runs out. Before you submit, it lists unanswered questions. When a quiz uses restrictions like Safe Exam Browser, or question types EM3 doesn't support yet, it explains why and keeps "Answer on E3".
- **Deadline reminders** (server version): get a browser push notification or a Discord webhook message before assignments and quizzes close, even when EM3 isn't open. Choose from 1 hour to 7 days ahead (up to 4), get reminded again after an extension, and send a test notification from the settings.
- **Sync across devices** (server version): course colours, timetable, comment snippets, language and other settings are the same on every device. Each setting keeps its newest change, so a device you haven't opened in a while won't bring back old settings.
- **Automatic NYCU timetable** (server version): class times and rooms come straight from the syllabus, and times you edit yourself take priority. Clear a course to remove it from the timetable, or press Reset to go back to the syllabus times.

### Teachers and TAs

- **Split the class among TAs** (server version): on the Teaching tab, assign students to each TA evenly by student ID, by ID range, or one by one. The grading workspace gains "Only my students", and the roster shows who grades each student.
- **Currently grading** (server version): the roster shows "So-and-so is grading", and saving a student someone else is grading asks you first.
- **Scheduled grade release** (server version): scores and comments are kept in EM3 first, where every TA can see them, then written to E3 all at once at the time you choose, so the whole class is notified together. You can also "Write now" early or cancel.
- **Grading reminders for TAs** (server version): when submissions are waiting to be graded, you're notified by push or Discord, just like deadline reminders.
- **Re-read E3 grades before saving** (both versions): if someone changed a grade after you opened it, EM3 asks before saving, so you don't overwrite each other.

## Improved

- Screenshots on the introduction page are scaled to twice their largest display size, cutting their total from 3.1 MB to 1.2 MB, so the page loads faster.
- The introduction, sign-in and settings pages now explain where each version keeps your data.

## Fixed

- NYCU room codes pasted from the syllabus no longer keep campus markers like `[GF]`.
- When two TAs graded the same student at once, the later save silently replaced the earlier score. EM3 now warns you first.

## Upgrade notes

- Users who are already signed in see the version picker the next time they open EM3. Until you choose, or if you pick "Decide later", nothing changes.
- On iPhone, add EM3 to the Home Screen before you can receive push notifications.
- Scheduled releases are checked every 15 minutes, so release times on :00, :15, :30 or :45 are the most precise.
