The first release of EM3: a faster, clearer Moodle front end. Open [e3.emtech.cc](https://e3.emtech.cc) and sign in with your school account.

## Features

### Students

- **Today**: this week's work in one sentence; the class in session with shortcuts to its materials, assignments and announcements and a countdown to the end of class; a week calendar, upcoming deadlines, recent materials and the latest announcements.
- **To-do**: every assignment and quiz this semester, grouped into "overdue but still open, today, tomorrow, later", with countdowns.
- **Calendar**: week, two-week and month views. Deadlines use course colours, and submitted ones are crossed out.
- **Announcements**: one inbox across courses. Read state is kept only in your browser.
- **Courses**: grouped by semester with automatic icons and colours. Rename, pin or hide them.
- **Materials**: folders with a single file are flattened, the newest is on top, and PDFs, images and videos preview in place. Tick several files to download them as one zip (large files stream straight to disk).
- **Submit**: drop files in, confirm, submit. Allowed types, counts and sizes are checked as you pick files.
- **Quizzes**: attempt history, per-question results and explanations, and copying questions to discuss with AI (when the teacher allows it).
- **Timetable**: enter NYCU syllabus time codes or click the grid. CCU class times come from Moodle automatically, and the left side groups periods into zones Ⅰ–Ⅴ like the school's timetable.
- **Mail**: read, search, filter by course and mark as unread NYCU and CCU in-site mail.

### Teachers and TAs

- **Teaching tab** and a "To grade" card on the home page: submission and grading progress counted from actual submissions, including students who submitted but whose enrolment has lapsed.
- **Grading workspace**: student IDs, filters, `J`/`K` to switch students, `⌘↵` to save and move on, and a comment snippet library.
- **Bulk tools**: import grades from CSV by student ID (UTF-8 and Big5), upload a feedback zip that is matched back to students, extend deadlines in one click, and copy the list of students who haven't submitted.
- **Post announcements**: a Milkdown editor (toolbar or Markdown) with autosaved drafts and a preview before posting.
- **Quiz builder**: a question editor that exports Moodle XML. **Write materials**: turn Markdown into a format you can paste into E3.

### Everywhere

- **Many schools**: NYCU, CCU, NTNU, NCCU, NCKU, MCU, NCNU, NUK and NUTN, or paste the Moodle URL of any other school. CCU uses the school's single sign-on (SSO).
- **Four languages**: Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese and Emoji. EM3 follows the system by default, and you can switch in the settings, on the sign-in page and on the introduction page. The English interface shows English course names first.
- **Accessibility**: aims for WCAG 2.1 AA and Taiwan's web accessibility guidelines at AA. Automated axe checks report zero violations across four languages, two themes and 22 pages. Single-key shortcuts can be turned off.
- **Keyboard**: `⌘K` jumps to any course, number keys switch pages, and `⌘/` lists every shortcut.
- **App**: install it to your Home Screen and see the last loaded content offline. Every daisyUI theme, plus EM3's own light and dark themes.
- **No back end**: your browser talks to your school's Moodle directly. Your password is used only to get a sign-in token.

## Fixed

- Bulk grade import and feedback upload showed success even when some requests failed.
- The feedback upload dialog said you could drop files in, but drag and drop wasn't wired up.
- The first Tab press skipped "Skip to main content", and keyboard focus was lost after switching languages.
- On the home page, the date and file size of "Recently updated materials" wrapped one character per line in narrow columns, and the right column of the student home page sat lower than the left ([#6](https://github.com/elvisdragonmao/EM3/pull/6)).
- Opening CCU mail didn't mark it as read, and when marking as read failed, the whole message failed to load.
- An SSO link could sign you in again within 15 minutes of use, even after signing out, and a failed sign-in left the link with its token in the address bar.
- Timetable: pressing Enter on a filled cell with no brush selected cleared that period; the keyboard focus ring was hidden behind course blocks; CCU times filled in from Moodle couldn't be removed.
- The announcements page scrolled horizontally at 320 px wide, and course names in "To grade" overlapped with increased letter spacing.
